# My ET: Business Impact Model

This document provides a quantified estimate of the business impact delivered by the "My ET" AI-native intelligence platform. The model focuses on three primary value drivers: **Time Saved**, **Productivity Gains (Cost Reduction)**, and **Opportunity Capture (Revenue Recovery)**.

## 1. Core Assumptions

To calculate the impact, we assume a standard user profile (e.g., an Investment Manager, Policy Analyst, or Corporate Executive):

*   **Daily News Consumption**: 45 minutes (searching, filtering, and reading multiple sources).
*   **Hourly Value**: $100/hour (fully loaded cost of a mid-to-senior professional).
*   **Work Days**: 250 days per year.
*   **Signal Criticality**: Missing one major market or policy signal per year costs an average of $50,000 in missed opportunity or delayed reaction.

---

## 2. Quantified Impact

### **A. Time Saved (Efficiency)**
The "My ET" agentic pipeline automates the ingestion, ranking, and synthesis of news, reducing the daily "time-to-insight" significantly.

*   **Manual Process**: 45 minutes/day.
*   **AI-Native Process**: 10 minutes/day (Personalized Briefing + AI Chat for deep dives).
*   **Daily Savings**: 35 minutes.
*   **Annual Savings**: 35 mins * 250 days = 8,750 minutes (~145 hours).
*   **Impact**: **~18 full work days recovered per year.**

### **B. Productivity Gains (Cost Reduction)**
By automating the "low-value" task of news filtering, professionals can focus on "high-value" decision-making.

*   **Annual Hours Saved**: 145 hours.
*   **Hourly Rate**: $100.
*   **Annual Productivity Gain**: 145 hours * $100 = **$14,500 per user/year.**

### **C. Opportunity Capture (Revenue Recovery)**
The "Portfolio Alert" and "Watchlist" features ensure that critical signals are never missed, even in a high-noise environment.

*   **Baseline**: 1 critical signal missed/year due to information overload.
*   **Recovery**: Agentic pipeline provides 100% coverage of priority entities.
*   **Estimated Value**: **$50,000 per year in recovered opportunity.**

## 4. Enterprise Scaling (100-User Deployment)

For a corporate or institutional deployment of 100 users, the impact scales linearly:

*   **Total Time Recovered**: 14,500 hours (~7.25 full-time equivalents).
*   **Total Financial Impact**: **$6.45 Million per year.**

## 5. Qualitative Benefits (The "Intangible" Edge)

Beyond the math, "My ET" provides several strategic advantages:
1.  **Reduced Cognitive Load**: Prevents "decision fatigue" caused by information overload.
2.  **Vernacular Accessibility**: Democratizes high-end business intelligence for non-native English speakers (Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati support).
3.  **Strategic Alignment**: Ensures the entire team is aligned on the same "Connected Dots" and "Thesis" signals.
4.  **Faster Reaction Time**: Real-time alerts allow for market moves ahead of the general public.
